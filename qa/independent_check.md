## Checked but not verifiable from the available sources (not counted as errors)
- S4 "two P donors 13.0 nm apart" (attributed to He2019): only the He2019 abstract is available, and it does not give the spacing. `notes/specs/landmarks.md` also records this as unverified.
- Ruess2004 (registration markers + STM–SEM) and Koch2019's use of natural Si: only abstracts or metadata are available. They are consistent with those, but not confirmed.
- Edlbauer2025 "SET 17 nm" and "gates 146.6 nm" come from the Supplementary Information. I relied on the verbatim SI quotes in `notes/specs/processors_geometry.md` (rows 81, 83) because the SI is not in the repo. Reiner2024 independently gives "~17 nm" for its own device.

- Wang2022 and Kiczynski2022 numbers.
- Schofield2025 §1/§2.2/§2.4/§3.2/§5.4 numbers.
- Kane2000: 100 Å, 1 T, 0.1 K. O'Brien2001 and Schofield2003: 20 nm, ~12 nm, ~1 nm, 4 V/1 nA.
- Ivie2021: 63 ± 10%, 150 °C/>1 L.
