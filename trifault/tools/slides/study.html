TriFault · SF Lean MeetupZhang et al., Beyond Compilation, 2026
[04:30–06:00] This is the Beyond Compilation study, Zhang et al., 2026. They gave a tool-augmented GPT-5.2 agent 227 graduate-level textbook statements to formalize. 87.2 percent compile: 198 of 227. Only 57.7 percent are faithful to the English: 131 of 227. So about 30 points of "it compiles" is not "it means the same thing". This is not one bad model. All 8 systems they tested have a gap, from 1.3 to 29.5 points. And this isn't judge noise: human review confirms at least 23.8 of those 29.5 points are real translation errors.
