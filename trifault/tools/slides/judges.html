[07:30–10:00] These are counts I pulled from the Beyond Compilation released files. First: 18 of the 54 compiling outputs that humans confirmed don't say what the English says got a perfect 10 out of 10 from Gemini-2.5-Pro. A perfect score on a wrong statement. Second: in the random audit, 15 of 53 items, 28 percent, the human reviewers didn't all agree. Experts disagree on meaning. Third: on those same 53 items, the two AI judges split on 5. So you can't just pick one judge, human or model, and call it ground truth. You need something that weighs judges against each other and knows how far to trust each one.
