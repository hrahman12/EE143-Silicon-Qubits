[39:00–40:30] The product loop. One: you play. A three-way vote, you tap the span of Lean that's wrong, and you pick a reason tag. Two: the p-trit engine turns all votes into a verdict with a confidence. Three: anything with low confidence goes to more players, like round 3 did. Four: Lean witnesses, which the kernel checks, and expert-confirmed items become gold items. Gold items score everyone, players and AI judges alike, and that's what the trust on the last slide is learned against. Every round played makes the dataset bigger and the trust estimates sharper.
