[46:00–47:30] What compounds, as a plan. The labeled set of real misformalizations. The reason tags, which say why each one is wrong. Every rater's learned trust, human and AI. And Lean-witnessed gold items, which the kernel has checked. Two lessons from earlier science games. Foldit, 2011: players solved the structure of a retroviral protease that had stumped scientists for over a decade, in three weeks. But DARPA's Verigames, which tried to turn software verification into games, got smaller audiences and more one-time players than regular games, per a 2014 Naval Postgraduate School study. So we don't design for the mass market. We design for power players: people like this room.
