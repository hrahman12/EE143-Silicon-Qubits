[30:00–31:00] Here's how Lean comes back in. Lean can't check meaning. But a player who votes "doesn't say it" can often settle the question with a Lean proof: that the statement is false, or trivially true, or true in a case where the English says it isn't. The kernel checks that proof, so it's gold. For round 3 the witness is the cyclic group of order 6: plug it in and both disjuncts fail. For round 1 it's the one-line proof that the statement holds without hA. Note for Hanah: the Witness.lean file in deck/lean has not been compiled yet, so the slide shows only the sentence.
