TriFault · SF Lean Meetup
[17:00–18:00] Here's the game. I show you an English statement and the Lean an AI agent wrote for it. You vote three ways. Says it: the Lean means what the English means. Can't tell: the English is ambiguous or you can't decide. Doesn't say it: the Lean means something else. Hands up for each option when I ask. In the product you also tap the exact span of Lean that's wrong and pick a reason, so a vote carries where and why, not just a verdict. Four rounds: a warm-up, round 2, a bonus, and the boss.
