[14:00–17:00] Who else is working on this, and they're good. Lean Atlas, 2026, builds a dependency graph for human meaning review. It cuts the nodes you have to check by 94 to 99 percent on proof-heavy projects, and 27.3 percent on a definition-heavy one. Numina Fuse, 2026: agents formalize, then reviewer agents and people check meaning. Automatic judges: LeanScorer reaches kappa 0.766 against human labels, versus 0.603 for a plain LLM judge. AWS Automated Reasoning checks turn natural-language policies into logic and can return a TRANSLATION_AMBIGUOUS finding. The gap column is my read of where each stops. All of these assume an expert, or one judge, is the last word. Bottom line: no game, and nothing that learns whom to trust. That's the space we take.
