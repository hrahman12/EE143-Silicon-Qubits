Short answers.

Isn't this just LLM-as-judge? The judges are inputs. The engine learns how far to trust each one from gold items and flags where they disagree. On the study's 120 audited statements, Gemini-2.5-Pro alone matches the human majority 75.0% of the time.

Why p-trits and not a plain Bayesian model? Mathematically it is Bayesian Dawid–Skene. Writing it as p-trits gives the same sampler TriFault runs, and that sampler runs natively on p-trit hardware.

Who labels the gold items? Lean witnesses, which the kernel checks, plus expert-confirmed mismatches.

How do you stop players from gaming it? Gold items score every player, and trust is learned per player.

What if the English is the problem? That's what “can't tell” is for: it flags the source, not the Lean.

Why would AI provers pay? Their outputs only count if the statements mean what they say. We see Harmonic and Axiom as potential partners and customers, not competitors.
