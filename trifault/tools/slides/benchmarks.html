[06:00–07:30] It's not just one study. miniF2F, the benchmark everyone reports on: Ospanov et al., 2025, found the formal and informal statements disagree on more than half the problems. And when you chain formalize-then-prove end to end you get about 36 percent, versus the 97 and 69 percent reported for the two steps separately. ProofNet#: Poiroux et al. found 31.8 percent of entries in the Lean 4 ports had formalization errors. Formal Conjectures has 2,615 Lean statements, 1,029 of them open problems. For the Erdős problem that Alexeev and Mixon resolved in 2025, the repo's Lean statement allowed modulus v equal to 0. These are careful teams. The problem is the task, not the people.
