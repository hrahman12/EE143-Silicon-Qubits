[42:00–43:00] One picture of the whole stack. On the left, the verdict network, which is software today. A p-trit in hardware would be this TiN, NbOx, TiN device: a 150-nanometre via and a 20-nanometre film. The film is NbO2, whose crystal has a 13.70 by 13.70 by 5.98 angstrom cell with 96 atoms in the conventional cell. And inside the crystal, one niobium chain: paired when insulating, 3.03 angstroms evenly spaced when metallic. That was the last ten slides in one strip. From a verdict about a Lean statement down to an atom. We're at the left end today.
