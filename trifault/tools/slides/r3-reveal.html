Click: ≃* ZMod (2 * p) is a multiplicative equivalence with ZMod's ring multiplication, not with the cyclic group of order 2p. In that monoid 0 has no inverse, so no group can be multiplicatively isomorphic to it. The left disjunct is always impossible, and the statement really claims every group of order 2p is dihedral. That's false: the cyclic group of order 6 is abelian, and D_3 isn't. The study's own two-stage model review flagged it; the human majority missed it.
