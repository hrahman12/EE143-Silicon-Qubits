[03:00–04:30] Everyone here knows the right half of this picture. The kernel checks that the proof proves the Lean statement, and it is very good at that. The left arrow is the one nobody checks mechanically: does the Lean statement mean what the English means? Today that check is a person reading carefully, or a model acting as a judge. If the formal statement is wrong, a perfect proof of it is a perfect proof of the wrong thing. Everything else in this talk is about that left arrow.
